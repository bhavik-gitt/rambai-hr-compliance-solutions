## About RAMBAI

**RAMBAI HR & COMPLIANCE SOLUTIONS** is focused on helping businesses manage their human resources, payroll operations, statutory obligations, and workforce compliance requirements.

The website presents RAMBAI's professional services through a modern, elegant, and user-friendly digital experience, helping businesses discover services, understand their requirements, and connect with the company for consultations.

Our approach is built around three principles:

- **People** — Supporting organisations through effective HR management and workforce solutions.
- **Compliance** — Helping businesses navigate statutory and labour law requirements.
- **Progress** — Enabling organisations to operate with greater confidence and efficiency.

## UI/UX Design Philosophy

The visual experience follows a premium corporate design direction, combining minimalism, clarity, elegant typography, and a refined colour palette.

### Brand Colour Palette

| Colour | Hex Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Emerald Green | `#003D35` | Primary brand identity |
| Dark Forest Green | `#002C26` | Deep backgrounds and accents |
| Metallic Gold | `#D4AF37` | Highlights and interactive accents |
| Warm Ivory | `#F8F6F0` | Soft background sections |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean content surfaces |

### Typography

- Elegant serif typography for prominent headings.
- Clean sans-serif typography for paragraphs and interface elements.
- Clear visual hierarchy across headings, descriptions, and actions.
- Consistent typography for a professional reading experience.

### Design Principles

- Minimal and uncluttered layouts.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.
- Clear navigation and page hierarchy.
- Premium card-based content presentation.
- Balanced use of whitespace.
- Accessible contrast and readable text.
- Consistent buttons, forms, and interactive elements.
